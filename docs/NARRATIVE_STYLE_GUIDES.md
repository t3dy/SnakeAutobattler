# Radiant Narrative Style Guides (v13.2)

> "The engine does not just calculate; it speaks." — The Archivist

This document contains **30 templates** and style guides to ensure consistency across the Snake Autobattler's narrative expansion. Use these when writing new `TraitTriggers`, `EncounterResults`, or `FlavorText`.

---

## I. Snake Identity & Voice

### 1. The Boulderback Voice (Body: High Size, Low Speed)
**Template**: "The {Snake} [Action Verb: lumbered, plowed, braced] through the {Terrain}, ignoring the [Minor Threat: thorns, heat, wind]."
**Guideline**: Focus on mass, momentum, and indifference to small harms. Never use "sprint" or "dodge."
**Example**: "The Boulderback plowed through the forest, ignoring the thorns that scraped its flank."

### 2. The Whipcoil Voice (Body: Low Size, High Speed)
**Template**: "A [Visual Metaphor: blur, ribbon, snap] of motion, the {Snake} [Action: flickered, vanished] before the {Threat} could react."
**Guideline**: Emphasize speed as a visual distortion. The snake is rarely seen clearly.
**Example**: "A blur of motion, the Whipcoil vanished before the trap could snap shut."

### 3. The Industrial Voice (Theme: Sci-Fi)
**Template**: "{Snake}'s [Part: optics, servos, plating] [Action: whined, cycled, readjusted] as it calculated the {Target}'s trajectory."
**Guideline**: Use mechanical/digital verbs. Pain is "damage," instincts are "algorithms."
**Example**: "Unit-734's optics cycled as it calculated the prey's trajectory."

### 4. The Organic Voice (Theme: Medieval/Nature)
**Template**: "The {Snake} tasted the air, its [Part: tongue, scales, muscle] [Action: twitching, sensing, bristling] with the scent of {Target}."
**Guideline**: Focus on sensory input—smell, heat, vibration. Primal and visceral.
**Example**: "The Serpent tasted the air, its scales bristling with the scent of fear."

### 5. The Cowardly Voice (Instinct: Survival)
**Template**: "{Snake} [Action: froze, flinched, recalculated] at the sound of {Threat}, seeking the nearest [Safe Spot: shadow, crack, darkness]."
**Guideline**: Heroism is a failure state. Survival is the only victory. Panic is near surface.
**Example**: "Snake-Eyes froze at the sound of thunder, seeking the nearest crack in the wall."

### 6. The Blood-Crazed Voice (Instinct: Aggression)
**Template**: "The metallic tang of {Target} drove the {Snake} into a [State: frenzy, focus, trance], [Action: disregarding, ignoring] its own safety."
**Guideline**: Tunnel vision. The goal is violence; the self is secondary.
**Example**: "The metallic tang of blood drove the Viper into a frenzy, disregarding the flames."

### 7. The Regal Voice (Trait: Gilded Hood)
**Template**: "With a [Adjective: haughty, disdainful] flare of its hood, the {Snake} [Action: demanded, permitted, dismissed] the presence of {Target}."
**Guideline**: The world exists for the snake's amusement. Combat is beneath it, yet necessary.

### 8. The Glitch Voice (Affinity: Void)
**Template**: "The {Snake} [Action: jagged, rendering-failed, no-clipped] through the {Terrain}, its form [Visual: flickering, desaturating, pixelating]."
**Guideline**: Break the fourth wall subtly. Physics are suggestions.

### 9. The Moss-Bound Voice (Affinity: Forest)
**Template**: "Rooted in the {Terrain}, the {Snake} seemed less a creature and more a [Noun: vine, branch, growth] given sudden [Noun: animus, hunger]."
**Guideline**: Blur the line between flora and fauna.

### 10. The Sun-Scorched Voice (Affinity: Desert)
**Template**: "Radiating [Noun: waves, stored heat], the {Snake} moved like [Metaphor: liquid glass, a mirage] across the dunes."
**Guideline**: Heat is power. Movement is fluid but intense.

## II. Encounter & Combat Choreography

### 11. The Ambush (Hunter vs. Prey)
**Template**: "Silence, then violence. The {Attacker} [Action: erupted, uncoiled] from the {Cover}, fangs finding {Defender}'s [Part: throat, flank]."
**Guideline**: Focus on the transition from stillness to chaos.

### 12. The Clash (Tank vs. Tank)
**Template**: "Scale ground against scale like [Sound: millstones, tectonic plates] as {Name1} and {Name2} met in a [Adjective: deadlock, stalemate]."
**Guideline**: Emphasize weight, friction, and lack of movement.

### 13. The Evasion (Speed vs. Power)
**Template**: "{Attacker}'s strike hit nothing but [Noun: air, dust, afterimage]. {Defender} was already [Location: gone, behind, circling]."
**Guideline**: The attacker looks foolish; the defender looks effortless.

### 14. The Tactical Retreat (Smart Exit)
**Template**: "{Snake} assessed the [Noun: odds, differential] and [Action: smoked, faded, withdrew], leaving the {Enemy} to strike at shadows."
**Guideline**: Retreating is a decision, not a failure.

### 15. The Desperate Flight (Panic Exit)
**Template**: "Scrabbling for purchase, {Snake} [Action: threw, flung] itself into the {Terrain}, putting raw distance between itself and death."
**Guideline**: Dignity is gone. Fear is the engine.

### 16. The Kill (Lethal Strike)
**Template**: "A single, [Adjective: decisive, surgical, brutal] puncture. {Victim}'s coil [Action: went slack, unspooled] as the venom took hold."
**Guideline**: Don't linger on gore; linger on the finality.

### 17. The Environmental Kill (Hazard Usage)
**Template**: "{Snake} [Action: maneuvered, baited] {Enemy} into the {Hazard}, letting the {Terrain} do the dirty work."
**Guideline**: Intelligence over brute force.

### 18. The Slapstick Bonk (Genre: Comedy)
**Template**: "With a resonant [Sound: DONK, CLANG, THWACK], {Snake} rebounded off the {Object}, eyes spinning like [Metaphor: slot machines, tops]."
**Guideline**: Cartoon physics. No permanent damage implies.

### 19. The Noir Monologue (Genre: Mystery)
**Template**: "\"It was a {Terrain} like any other,\" mused {Snake}, \"until the {Enemy} walked in with a smile like a {Metaphor}.\""
**Guideline**: First-person internal monologue. Cynical, weary.

### 20. The Zombie Infection (Genre: Horror)
**Template**: "{Snake} didn't feel the bite, only the [Sensation: cold, spreading numbness] that followed. Calculations began to return: [Result: NULL]."
**Guideline**: Body horror mixed with system failure.

## III. Environmental & Atmospheric

### 21. Forest Atmosphere (Lush)
**Template**: "Canopy shadows stretched like [Metaphor: fingers, webs], hiding the {Threat} that waited in the rot."
**Guideline**: Claustrophobic, damp, alive.

### 22. Desert Atmosphere (Arid)
**Template**: "The sun was a [Noun: hammer, tyrant], beating the {Terrain} into a flat, shimmering submission."
**Guideline**: Oppressive brightness, silence, exposure.

### 23. River Atmosphere (Hydro)
**Template**: "The current pulled with [Adjective: deceptive, gentle] strength, threatening to wash the {Snake} downstream."
**Guideline**: Constant motion, cold, slick surfaces.

### 24. Mountain Atmosphere (Elevation)
**Template**: "The air was thin and [Adjective: sharp, biting]. Every movement cost twice the energy in the {Terrain}."
**Guideline**: Verticality, isolation, clarity.

### 25. Cyber-City Atmosphere (Sci-Fi)
**Template**: "Neon rain slicked the {Terrain}, reflecting the [Color] advertisement for [Product] above."
**Guideline**: Sensory overload, artificiality, commerce.

### 26. The Storm Edge (Phase 2 Warning)
**Template**: "The horizon dissolved into white static. A low [Sound/Hum] vibrated in {Snake}'s bones."
**Guideline**: Existential dread. The erasing of reality.

### 27. The Glitch Zone (Corruption)
**Template**: "Geometry folded wrong here. The {Object} looked [Adjective: 2D, untextured] and smelled like [Noun: ozone, burnt toast]."
**Guideline**: Wrongness. Senses lying.

## IV. Meta & UI Feedback

### 28. Radiance Shift (Up)
**Template**: "The narrative [Action: snaps, aligns] into focus. A golden hue washes over the UI."
**Guideline**: Clarity, harmony, purpose.

### 29. Radiance Shift (Down)
**Template**: "Entropy bleeds in. The text begins to [Action: smear, drift]. Meaning is lost."
**Guideline**: Confusion, decay, static.

### 30. The Victory Lap (Win)
**Template**: "{Snake} remains. The timeline solidifies around their victory, archiving this reality."
**Guideline**: Permanence. The end of the loop.
