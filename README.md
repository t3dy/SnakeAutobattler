# 🐍 Snake Autobattler: The Math-to-Myth Chronicles

> *"The soil does not remember the victor, but the chronicler remembers the struggle."*

Welcome to a persistent experiment in **procedural narrative synthesis**. This is not just a game; it is an evolution of eight specialized engines, each advancing the art of turning simulation data into meaningful, dramatic history.

## 🌟 The Vision
The Snake Autobattler project explores the **Math-to-Myth** boundary. We believe that every point of damage, every coordinate shifted, and every "Reckless" choice made by a simulation unit should contribute to a lush, thematic story. 

What started as simple terminal logs has evolved into a "Living Chronicle" where SVG morphing, climatic particle systems, and autonomous personality-driven resolve create a window into another world.

## 🚀 The Evolutionary Roadmap
Witness eight generations of software engineering and game design:

*   **v1.0 - v3.0: The Foundation** (Primal logs, Identity traits, and the first visual Emoji Arena).
*   **v4.0 - v5.0: The Story Compiler** (Arc-based narration and the shift to Medieval/Sci-Fi thematic layers).
*   **v6.0 - v7.0: The Choice Pivot** (Manual FTL-style agency expanding into Autonomous Resolve based on character trauma).
*   **v8.0: The Living Chronicle (New!)** (Procedural cinematic zoom, SVG snake morphing, and atmospheric weather layering).

## 🛠️ The Tech Stack (Evolutionary Meta)
- **Engine Core**: TypeScript & React (v3.0+ transition).
- **Procedural Narrative**: The custom `StoryCompiler` (v4.0+).
- **Visual Synthesis**: CSS Grid + High-performance SVG layers (v8.0).
- **Versioning Control**: A strict adherence to architectural history, allowing you to "Select Engine" from the digital archive.

## 📖 The "Overseer" Experience
You are the **Overseer**. You draft the brood, you define their quirks, and then—depending on the version—you either command their every move or watch their inherent resolve play out against the backdrop of a closing storm.
