# 🐍 Snake Autobattler: The Radiant Engine (v13.0)

> *"The soil does not remember the victor, but the chronicler remembers the struggle."*

Welcome to a persistent experiment in **procedural narrative synthesis**. This is not just a game; it is an evolution of thirteen specialized engines, each advancing the art of turning simulation data into meaningful, dramatic history.

## 🌟 The Radiant Evolution

### v13.0: The Radiant Architect (Latest)
The "Meta-Agentic" leap. We've introduced the **Hall of Designers**—18 specialized AI-representative personas who audit and influence every tick of the engine.
- **The Problem**: Solving "Mechanical Drift" (where simulation and narrative diverge).
- **The Tool**: The **Radiance Score** and **Synergy Blueprint**, ensuring that every trait activation is felt across all game pillars.

### The "Problem -> Tool" Journey
- **The Problem**: Narrative felt detached from the math.
  - **The Tool**: `v4.0 Saga Engine` introduced a dramatic-arc classifier to group raw events into meaningful story beats.
- **The Problem**: Manual agency was tedious in an autobattler.
  - **The Tool**: `v7.0 Personality Matrix` automated decision-making based on trait-driven causality.
- **The Problem**: "Orphan Traits" caused systemic inconsistency.
  - **The Tool**: `RadiantToy` (v12.0) created a feedback loop for interactive mechanical auditing.

## 🛠️ Architecture

## Technical Philosophy: The "Radiant" Evolution
Our design principles have evolved from **Simple Emission** to **Dynamic Synergy**.

1.  **From Logs to Legends (v1-v4)**: We moved from printing numbers to grouping events into narrative arcs.
2.  **From Control to Character (v5-v8)**: We realized that players care more about "why" a snake acted than "how" to win.
3.  **From Content to Blueprint (v9-v13)**: The current era focuses on the **Infrastructure of Inconsistency**. We now build tools (like the Radiant Toy) to find where our simulation fails our narrative, treating these "Lacunas" as the primary gameplay loop for the "Architect" player.

**Access to Technology**: The shift from raw TypeScript logic to **React-based Meta-Dashboards** has allowed us to visualize the *engine itself* as a playable surface.
