# Design Logs: Narrative Logic & Experiments

## Experiment 1: The "Dread Log" vs. "The Story"
**Logic**: Coordinate logging is boring; we need terrain-aware narration.

## Experiment 2: Trait-Driven Pathfinding (v4.0)
**Logic**: Quirks should affect movement, not just stats.

## Experiment 3: The Interrupted Loop (v6.0)
**Logic**: Break "Passive Fatigue" by forcing choices.

## Experiment 4: Autonomous Resolve (v7.0)
**Logic**: The "Ghost in the Scales." Automating choices based on trauma creates a deeper connection to the unit's "Will."

## Experiment 5: Visual Synthesis (v8.0)
**Logic**: Does the visual style affect how the story is read?
**Approach**: By moving from static emojis to **Kinetic SVG Morphing** and **Cinematic Zoom**, we emphasize the "Scene-First" nature of our history engine. The camera no longer watches a board; it watches a *protagonist*.
**Result**: Highly increased player immersion during "Causal Cascades." The screen-flash on impact makes the math feel "heavy."
